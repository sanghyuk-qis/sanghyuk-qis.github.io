Experimental AMO Physics

Sang Hyuk Hong

I am an AMO experimentalist interested in quantum metrology and quantum computation. I received my B.S. in Physics from Korea University and am currently a Research Assistant at the Quantum Gas Lab, Seoul National University.

At the Agency for Defense Development, I worked on optically pumped magnetometers and Rb–Xe nuclear magnetic resonance gyroscopes. This work shaped my interest in how experimental design and control of systematic effects determine measurement performance.

At Seoul National University, I contribute to the construction of an Rb–Yb mixed-species neutral-atom tweezer array platform for quantum information science, extending my experience from vapor-cell sensors to individually controlled atoms. For my Ph.D., I aim to build on this foundation in quantum metrology, with particular interests in optical clocks and quantum computational sensing.
